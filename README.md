# Abdelsadeq Elfergany

PhD student and researcher at the [Information & Computing Lab (ICLAB)](https://iclab.uvigo.gal/), atlanTTic Research Center, Universidade de Vigo.

I work on **continual learning** for 5G/6G networks: models that keep learning as traffic and attacks change, without forgetting what they already know. My earlier work covers swarm intelligence for recommendation (CyberSwarm) and deep learning for online community analysis. I also build applied generative-AI systems: retrieval-augmented question answering, LLM pipelines for educational video, and prompt-injection detection.

## Selected publications

- **Cyberswarm: a novel swarm intelligence algorithm inspired by cyber community dynamics.** *Artificial Intelligence Review* 58, 406 (2025). [doi:10.1007/s10462-025-11406-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11406-4)
- **Content-driven cyber community identification using deep learning.** *Journal of Big Data* 13, 87 (2026). [doi:10.1186/s40537-026-01472-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-026-01472-y)
